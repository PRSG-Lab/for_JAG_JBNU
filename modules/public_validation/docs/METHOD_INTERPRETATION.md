# Method interpretation

The archive-based analysis evaluates agreement with an edge-independent reference route. The reference route is not assumed error-free. Its conservative variance is added to the reported candidate-route variance when forming the comparison interval.

The fitted quantities are an effective nominal variance scale and an effective route-coherent scale. They are not claimed to identify a specific physical mechanism such as refraction or staff-scale error.

`GammaFixed = 1` is a normalisation. The empirically fitted scale is carried by `kappa`; the workflow does not claim separate identification of Gamma and a common scale multiplier.
